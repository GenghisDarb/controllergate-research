A topology candidate, source-contact hypothesis, shadow requirement, or typed incident does not authorize a patch. Source ownership requires candidate-specific executed evidence and independent verification. Patch actuation additionally requires separate human authorization and the complete repair-license chain.

Safe abstention is a valid result: when evidence cannot discriminate among legal causal hypotheses, ControllerGate records the blocker and reopen condition without manufacturing a terminal or patch.
