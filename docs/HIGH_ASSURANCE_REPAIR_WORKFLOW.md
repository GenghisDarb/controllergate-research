# High-assurance repair workflow

ControllerGate separates acquisition, observation, causal diagnosis, authority, actuation, and release state.

1. Freeze an exact candidate contract without truth, repair outcomes, or patch bytes.
2. Materialize source, build, provider, execution, target, and truth custody in six disjoint compartments.
3. Measure tracked source and test identities from Git objects; classify generated residue separately.
4. Capture process, structured test, warning, import, provider, service, product, integrity, and trace observations.
5. Independently verify the typed incident product.
6. Compile a candidate-specific causal-contact hypergraph from raw source/runtime parents.
7. Derive legal probes from unresolved graph cells and choose a deterministic minimax probe unless calibrated likelihoods exist.
8. Maintain provisional facts, contradictions, failed branches, and nogoods. Only ControllerAudit may commit or abstain.
9. Produce source-ownership evidence through executed stages and distinct verifiers.
10. Require separate human authorization before any protected actuation.
11. Validate source-only change, unchanged tests, duplicate clean replay, rollback, and count uniqueness.
12. Generate public state from scoped current evidence, never from an old summary.

An upstream block stops its dependent subtree. ControllerGate preserves the evidence, exact blocker, and reopen condition; it does not emit downstream success placeholders.
