## Functional correspondence

The proposed connection to Reactome concerns design, functional logic, dynamics and environment relationships. It is not a hypothesis that biological terminology alone adds predictive power. A neutral-label control can test label dependence; it does not exhaust the functional hypothesis.

A declared state/action mapping should preserve the required enabling conditions and the relevant successor state. A time-dependent or stochastic claim additionally requires the corresponding time, rate or probability structure. Reactome annotation-to-model fidelity, model-to-code fidelity and model-to-cell validity are separate obligations.

## Ten-engine / one-coupling / three-shell

The project uses a 10/1+3 engine/coupling/shell interpretation of its fourteen-layer architecture. These are intended as cascading complexity or functional organization, not a claim of fourteen ordinary spatial directions.

The small 23 November reaction model has ten compartment-qualified metabolite places and three aggregate active-catalyst assertions. A categorical exclusion of any architectural reading of those quantities was too strong. An engine/coupling/shell mapping may be investigated, but it requires typed functional correspondence and preservation tests. Adding a coupling operation to thirteen places is not by itself a demonstration of fourteen independent state variables or a unique natural layer count.

The three catalytic roles are LDH, GAPDH and an active BSG:MCT transport complex. Their being three does not decide whether they instantiate an upper shell, another triad, or merely three selected catalytic channels. Historical definitions and experiments must decide the proposed role correspondence. Distinct seed, observer, operator and architectural triads must not be silently merged.

## Closure and observation

The broader project vocabulary permits multiple forms and levels of closure. Operational ladder closure is one specified diagnostic. Other claims can concern local recurrence, projected return, invariant/resource conservation, interlocked conditions, observational indistinguishability, or closure relative to an intervention and environment.

For each use, identify the state being tested, observation/projection, transition or intervention, system boundary, reference state or invariant, tolerance, and time/depth window. A passive change of observation and an operation that changes the underlying state are different claims even when they produce similar reported outputs.

The preserved two-reaction calculation gives an exact example: the NAD+/NADH/H+ projection returns after a valid GAPDH/LDH pair while the complete material state changes. Its calculations remain valid under their specified assumptions. Calling it a lower or projected closure within the project does not automatically validate a higher ladder criterion or a physical observer-caused process.

## Evidence and synergy

Several components can acquire value through a tested interaction even when each component uses known mathematics. A combined result must nevertheless specify the joint target, the actual coupling, what changes under component removal, and an appropriate baseline. Intersecting evidence gates is a logical combination; it is not automatically superadditive predictive gain. Preserve both the legitimate synergy hypothesis and the measurements needed to establish its particular form.
