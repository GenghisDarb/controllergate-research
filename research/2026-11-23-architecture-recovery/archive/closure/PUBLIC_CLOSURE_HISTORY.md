## The supported interpretation

The recovered history supports a broader TORUS concept of closure than the particular ladder endpoint defined in the formal TLD lexicon. Earlier work discusses local convergence, global constraint acceptance, observer stabilization, state or phase return, and agreement across interacting structures. The formal ladder definition supplies a specific operational diagnostic within that larger research program. [C01,C06,C08,C10–C13,C20–C25]

A biological reaction model that preserves coupled resource use, conservation and partial return can therefore have a legitimate TORUS interpretation. Establishing a particular layer mapping requires an additional account of which roles and relationships are preserved. Counts of resources, catalysts or software services do not establish that correspondence by themselves. They also do not independently establish physical dimensionality or a uniquely necessary fourteen-layer model.

## Observation and closure need explicit types

Passive observation is a readout (H(x)). A physical observation interaction or controller intervention is instead part of a state transition. An unread coupled subsystem may affect a measured subsystem even without a recorded outcome. Conversely, recording or projecting an already available state in software need not change it. Registry labels separating observed data from null simulations address a different question again. These distinctions are supported by the original observer mathematics and later project definitions. [C01–C05,C20–C24]

| Endpoint | Meaning | What it does not automatically establish |
|---|---|---|
| Full-state return | The declared complete state returns to its initial value. | Primitive period, attraction or uniqueness of the model. |
| Projected or subsystem return | A specified readout or subset of coordinates returns. | Return of hidden coordinates, unless the readout is injective on the admissible state family. |
| Invariant preservation | A conserved quantity remains unchanged. | Completion of a cycle; the state may continue changing. |
| Quiescence | No allowed action is enabled. | Successful completion, return or stability; a necessary resource may simply be absent. |
| Attracting fixed point | A fixed state exists and a separately stated convergence condition holds. | Physical correctness of the dynamics or a uniquely selected layer count. |
| Interlocked constraint satisfaction | Several named conditions hold simultaneously. | Existence or uniqueness of a solution, a coupled evolution law, or causal validation. |
| Formal TLD ladder closure | The frozen ladder operator, admissibility and terminal/reference criterion meet the declared stability conditions. | Every other meaning of closure, or physical laws without domain translation. |
| Null separation and survival | A frozen diagnostic distinguishes the input from matched nulls over a declared axis and persistence condition. | Full-state return or physical time without further identification. |

Some predicates imply others: full return implies deterministic projected return. Others address different questions rather than forming a universal hierarchy. Reports should name the state, permitted actions, readout, criterion, tolerance and sampling unit used.

## The recovered observer repair, stated precisely

For the historical interaction (H_{\mathrm{int}}=J|1\rangle\langle1|\otimes Q), an ancilla in a zero-eigenvalue state of (Q) produces no conditional phase. A superposition of eigenstates with eigenvalues zero and one changes that result. With \(\theta=Jt/\hbar\), the two conditional ancilla states have overlap

\[
\gamma=\frac{1+e^{-i\theta}}{2}.
\]

Tracing out that ancilla gives normalized balanced-fringe visibility

\[
V=|\gamma|=\left|\cos\frac{\theta}{2}\right|
 =1-\frac{\theta^2}{8}+O(\theta^4)
\]

near zero. A visibility loss of one part per million therefore requires \(\theta\approx\sqrt{8\times10^{-6}}\approx0.002828\). The reduced-state offdiagonal magnitude is \(|\gamma|/2\), which is distinct from the normalized visibility. This preserves the genuine historical repair while correcting its remaining normalization and arithmetic errors. It uses two eigenstates and does not identify fourteen observer states. [C02–C05]

A nonselective dephasing channel also retains every diagonal density matrix; it is not automatically a strict contraction to one pure state. Strict contraction can justify a unique attracting fixed point only for the specified complete update map and state space. If feedback depends on memory, that memory belongs in the state. [C01]

## What combining evidence can add

The historical pairwise proxy compares the joint impact with the worse single impact. A positive value does not by itself establish nonadditivity: effects of two and three units that add to five still exceed the worse single effect. An additive interaction contrast instead compares all four conditions on a declared outcome scale:

\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{interaction}}=Y_{11}-Y_{10}-Y_{01}+Y_{00}.
\]

The appropriate contrast, sampling units and uncertainty depend on the task. Saturation and compensation also matter. The later project discussion already recognizes that combinations can compensate or duplicate one another. [C14,C15,C17]

Three different combined benefits should remain distinct:

- Several logical checks can jointly exclude a state that each check individually accepts.
- Complementary observations can jointly identify changes that individual readouts miss.
- A combined design can improve predictive or control performance on held-out measurements.

The first two can be established for a formal model through proof and computation. The third requires comparative empirical evidence. Several projections of one source remain related observations, even when their combination is useful.
