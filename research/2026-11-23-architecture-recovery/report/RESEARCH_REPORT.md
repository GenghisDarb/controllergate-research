# Architecture, observation and ghost constants: recovery and executed extension

Date: 23 November 2026. Evidence status: historical reconstruction, exact model reasoning and newly executed bounded software checks. This report does not certify a production release, a biological mechanism, a unique fourteen-layer theory or a new fundamental physical law.

## 1. The correction

The previous categorical statement that ten compartment-qualified metabolite places and three active-catalyst assertions have no TLD interpretation was too strong. The project explicitly proposes an engine/coupling/shell architecture, uses layers as functional or complexity organization, and has a broader history of closure than one later ladder diagnostic. The relevant question is which functions and relationships a proposed correspondence preserves.

The correction is substantive. It does not require identifying every group of three with the same triad. In the recovered architecture, levels 0–9 supply ten engine layers, level 10 is a coupling boundary, and levels 11–13 form a three-role shell. The three catalytic assertions in the small reaction model are LDH, GAPDH and an active BSG:MCT transport complex. Those are distinct biochemical permissions; they are not already documented implementations of the shell's proposal, reconciliation and certification functions.

The new work therefore takes the historical architecture seriously enough to implement a specific role-inspired factorization and test its behavior. Its shell services are separate from catalyst context. The resulting implementation preserves the selected model's transitions throughout the tested domain. A generic interpreter with the same source information also passes, so a unique architectural advantage is not established.

The earlier reports and frozen numerical results are retained. The explicit correction record and this report supersede their categorical interpretation, rather than silently changing historical bytes.

## 2. What the recovered layer history actually says

### Engine, coupling and shell

The recovered Archive 9 discussion assigns a unifying throat to level 10 and S/R/G roles to levels 11/12/13. S proposes hypothetical trajectories; R reconciles them for consistency; G certifies legality relative to the modeled constraints. A later ControllerGate account explicitly gives the 0–9 / 10 / 11–13 partition. Its emergent controller is described as an interaction of roles rather than an extra fifteenth coordinate.

This is evidence of the project's intended architecture. It is not a proof that every reaction system has fourteen preferred levels, or that any ten measured quantities instantiate ten specific historical layers. A layer-by-layer map needs each layer's role, inputs, outputs and preserved relations. The implemented extension supplies one functional factorization; it does not fill that entire historical mapping automatically.

### Gravity and the order of constants

The detailed TORUS constants table places gravity at level 9, after an older overview placed it at level 6. The detailed sequence is alpha; Planck time, length and mass; light speed; Planck's constant; Boltzmann constant, Avogadro constant and gas constant; gravity; Planck temperature; unified coupling; cosmic length; cosmic time. The archival notation for Planck's constant must be retained as written; h and hbar are not interchangeable numerical values.

The placement has a documented descriptive rationale. The detailed account treats level 8 as completion of bulk thermodynamics and level 9 as gravitational organization, where thermal pressure and gravitational attraction jointly constrain structure. Level 10 then combines these anchors at the Planck-temperature threshold. This rationale appears in the constants treatise at physical lines 798–809 and Archive 3 at 19668–19686. It is more specific than a count preference, but no experiment in those passages establishes that position 9 is uniquely necessary. The archive also retains a competing draft table with G at 5 and a Hubble parameter at 9 (Archive 3:19977), before later detailed material returns to G at 9. The public historical ledger records these surviving alternatives instead of treating every draft as one consistent final table.

An especially useful recovered passage explicitly distinguishes **level 10's unification energy scale from level 11's coupling strength**. That repairs part of the apparent contradiction between a level-10 coupling role and the appearance of a unified-coupling constant at level 11. A functional boundary and the quantity used to characterize an adjacent layer are different entries in the account. The source supplies this distinction; it should not be presented as a newly invented reconciliation.

Order can matter whenever later operations use earlier outputs or when operators do not commute. However, changing a label or sequence alone does not establish that gravity must physically appear ninth. Planck units at lower indices already depend algebraically on G, hbar and c; Planck temperature additionally depends on kB, and R = NA kB. Accordingly, these anchors are interdependent. They can describe recursive relationships, but they cannot be counted as fourteen independently born constants or fourteen independent confirmations of the framework.

The historical records support restoring the intended assignment and distinguishing old and later tables. A demonstrated physical necessity for the precise order remains a separate claim.

### Distinct triads

The project contains several triads: seed constants, thermal constants, observer/operator triples, S/R/G shell roles and later control parameters. Their common cardinality does not make them identical. A useful correspondence must preserve a typed relationship: for example, proposing a transition, reconciling resources and certifying the resulting state. This is how the new shell is specified, rather than relabeling three enzymes as three shell layers.

## 3. Closure and observation: preserving the broader idea precisely

The recovered history supports several closure questions. Local workers can stop at diminishing returns while a controller separately checks global constraints. A projection can return while the complete state changes. A coupled system can satisfy joint constraints that separate components fail. The later TLD ladder endpoint is one defined operational test within this wider program.

The recovery also found later repairs: an initially ineffective observer ancilla was changed to a superposition; computational ToT work followed conceptual proposals; and subsequent XIV work separated onset lag, surviving lag and RMS argmin rather than treating them as one endpoint. These advances must not be erased by stopping an audit at the first incomplete artifact.

| Closure question | Checkable condition | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Full state returns | Final state equals its reference | Does not alone establish stability or primitive period |
| A view returns | Selected observations equal their reference | Hidden coordinates can still change |
| Resources remain conserved | Named invariants remain constant | Motion can continue within the invariant class |
| The process stops | No allowed transition is enabled | Starvation or missing catalyst can cause stopping |
| A fixed point attracts | Fixed-point condition plus an attraction argument | A fixed point alone does not prove attraction |
| Interlocked constraints close | All stated predicates hold together | Existence and uniqueness require additional evidence |
| A ladder endpoint is met | The actual ladder operator and its frozen endpoint test pass | Other forms of closure do not automatically imply it |

These are sometimes nested and sometimes different questions. Calling them all a single low-to-high hierarchy would conceal real distinctions. The useful generalization is a closure contract: state, observer, transition, boundary, reference, tolerance and evaluation window.

Passive readout H(x) does not change x. An active control or a physical system–ancilla interaction can change a state. An unread physical interaction differs from absence of interaction. The historical quantum calculation can be repaired explicitly: for an ancilla initially in a superposition, gamma = (1 + exp(-i theta))/2, so balanced fringe visibility is |cos(theta/2)|, approximately 1 - theta^2/8. This supports the specific conditional channel calculation, not a fourteen-state quantum theorem.

## 5. A concrete combined result: views jointly recover what each misses

The selected observers read substrate GA3P_c, redox-associated PYR_c and external lactate LACT_e. For each observer combination, the experiment counts ordered pairs of distinct reachable full states with the same readout. Such a pair witnesses that apparent projected return can conceal full-state change. This is an identifiability count, not a measured recurrence frequency.

| Readouts used | Ambiguous ordered pairs: 18-state system | Ambiguous ordered pairs: 6-state system |
|---|---:|---:|
| Substrate | 92 | 8 |
| Redox-associated | 88 | 8 |
| Boundary | 104 | 20 |
| Substrate + redox-associated | 26 | 2 |
| Substrate + boundary | 26 | 4 |
| Redox-associated + boundary | 22 | 4 |
| All three | **0** | **0** |

Every single readout and every pair leaves ambiguity. All three together identify the complete metabolite state within the declared conservation class. This is a real combined benefit, not merely three favorable descriptions of the same result.

The reason is exact. Write B for the three independent forward stoichiometric columns and xi for net reaction extents. All allowed metabolite differences satisfy delta x = B xi. Selecting the three readouts gives C B = diag(-1,-1,+1). Thus C delta x = 0 forces xi = 0, and hence delta x = 0. Conversely, full return implies every projected return. The proof is conditional on a fixed conservation class. Full-state recovery including catalytic roles additionally requires known, fixed catalytic context.

The ten metabolite coordinates have stoichiometric rank three and seven independent linear conservation relations. This fact does not invalidate a ten-layer functional interpretation; coordinate count, independently variable directions and complexity layers are different concepts. It does prevent treating the count ten as evidence of ten independent physical degrees of freedom.

The independent review tested all 120 possible triples of metabolite coordinates. **Seventy** have full rank on the stoichiometric span. The chosen triad is sufficient, not uniquely selected. No conclusion that one particular historical triad or fourteen-layer decomposition is necessary follows from this experiment.

### The hidden-context check

The experiment also turned all active-catalyst assertions off as an explicit external intervention. Metabolite readouts did not change, while the full state did and all six reactions became disabled. These catalyst-off states are outside the reachable class generated by the six reactions alone.

This supplies a useful diagnosis: a controller can mistake unchanged outputs for successful closure if it fails to track the context that determines future transitions. The remedy is to include the relevant context in the state or observe it separately. It is not evidence that passive observation caused the intervention.

## 6. Ghost constants: recovered substance and necessary correction

The ghost-constant program was present and substantial enough to require an actual mathematical audit. The Symposium defines an early inverse-gap score, later proposes eta(i)^24, j(i), an Igusa chi10 value and geometric-mean descendants, and sketches a partial layer map. The map fills the early layers and leaves later entries as placeholders. It is a historical research construction, not a completed fourteen-layer derivation.

The accessible continuation was searched for later corrections before judging these definitions. The broad search covered 781 distinct local text/code/notebook files; a semantic follow-up inspected 75 unique notebooks containing 1,403 cells, using source cells and text outputs rather than image/base64 data. These are overlapping search scopes. The substantive modular definitions were concentrated in the Symposium and repetitions. No later correction to the two numerical errors below was recovered in that accessible material. This is a bounded retrieval statement, not proof that no correction exists anywhere else.

### Values checked directly

| Archived definition | Archived value | Result under that definition |
|---|---:|---:|
| eta(i)^24 | about 0.003917 | **0.0017853698506421519043…** |
| j(i), with j = 1728 J | 1728 | **1728**, confirmed |
| chi10(diag(i,i)) | about 0.001867 | **0 exactly** |

The eta value was checked at 100-digit precision using the infinite product, the Gamma-function formula and a theta identity. The product and Gamma results differ by about 7.1e-102 at that working precision. The archived eta number is roughly 2.194 times the defined value.

The chi10 correction is structural. At a block-diagonal genus-two period matrix, one of the ten even theta constants factors into two odd elliptic theta constants at zero. That factor vanishes, so their squared product vanishes. Multiplication by any nonzero normalization cannot turn zero into the archived positive value. The number exp(-2 pi), approximately 0.0018674427, is related to a nome convention; it is not chi10 at the stated point.

The geometric-mean descendants involving that zero also become zero under the same definitions. Retaining the old positive descendants would require a changed model, explicitly versioned and retested. A different period matrix or definition may be scientifically useful, but cannot be treated as a silent repair that preserves the old numerical claims.

### A positive reconstruction also succeeded

The displayed weighted partition recipe was recomputed:

    S_N = 1/24 - sum(n=1..N) [1 + 0.5 cos(log n) + 0.1 log n] log(1-exp(-n)).

At N = 50, the new calculation gives **1.0439352679994650843…**, compared with the archived **1.0439352679994645**. The difference is about 5.84e-16. This is a reproducible piece of the historical computation and deserves credit.

However, this fixed recipe's increments at n = 10 and n = 14 are about 4.07e-5 and 6.87e-7. They do not support the separate claimed spectral matches near 0.0027 and 0.0022 at those positions. Later adjustable weights require their own frozen parameters before such a claim can be reproduced. Successful recovery of a sum does not validate every interpretation attached to it.

### A corrected mathematical route was actually evaluated

Set Omega(z) = [[i,z],[z,i]] for real z and define P10 explicitly as the raw product of the ten even theta constants squared. Then P10(0) = 0, and near zero its magnitude has the leading form

    |P10(z)| = [2^14 pi^2 eta(i)^48] z^2 + O(z^4).

The coefficient is approximately 0.5154375783. Direct finite theta sums give |P10(z)|/z^2 approximately 0.5154360 at z = 0.001 and 0.5152830 at z = 0.01. Enlarging the numerical summation window at z = 0.01 changes the result by about 1.2e-101 at the working precision.

This converts an incorrect nonzero seed into a mathematically consistent example of a quantity vanishing at decoupling and responding to coupling. The separating behavior is known mathematics; its proposed use inside TORUS would be a new modeling choice. No data-derived map from a biological or ControllerGate state to Omega(z) has been established, so this is not yet a biological predictor, a ghost constant discovered in nature or evidence selecting fourteen.

## 7. What synergy adds, and what it does not

The historical pair-removal proxy was pair impact minus the worse single impact. That can be positive even under exact additivity: single effects two and three, combined effect five, produce a positive proxy two. Later archives also recognize compensation and redundancy, which should be preserved rather than erased.

Three distinct kinds of combined value are now separated:

1. **Logical combination:** several checks together reject an invalid candidate that one check would admit.
2. **Observational complementarity:** jointly informative views remove ambiguity. The new experiment proves this for the specified model class.
3. **Predictive or control improvement:** a combined design performs better on held-out observations or interventions than matched alternatives. This still requires comparative evidence.

The first two are concrete progress. They do not automatically deliver the third. Similarly, the corrected modular coupling example and the complementary-observer result share a useful theme but are not independent evidence for one universal physical mechanism.

## 8. Publication assessment and current standing

The strongest supported paper direction is an integrated methods case study: source-grounded reaction-to-controller translation, typed closure, hidden-context controls, independent verification and explicit provenance of corrections. The previous preserved work adds mechanism ablations, shared-resource composition, pipeline integration checks and a separate limited real-data observation-model comparison. Together these are a serious, reproducible research package.

The current evidence supports publishing the methods, bounded results, failure diagnoses and limitations. It does not yet support announcing a uniquely necessary fourteen-layer structure, proven biological performance superiority, a universal ghost-constant spectrum, or fundamental physical unification. The elementary observability proof and corrected special-function values are useful components; they are not individually major new mathematical discoveries. Any novelty claim for the integrated method requires a documented comparison with prior methods; integration alone does not establish novelty. Comparative predictive or control gains remain a separate empirical objective. The package can serve as a reproducible methods case study with these boundaries visible.

Confidence has increased in three specific places: the recovered architecture has more precise historical content than the prior assessment credited; the broad closure/observer interpretation has real mathematical and executable examples; and progress can be preserved with source and test custody rather than conversational memory. Confidence decreases for the uncorrected modular ghost numbers. Honest advancement needs both movements.
