Functional correspondence: precise claims and limits
===================================================

Purpose
-------
Brad's hypothesis concerns preservation of functional relationships, dynamics,
and environmental constraints. Label invariance is a useful sanity check, but
does not test that hypothesis. This note gives an operational definition that
can be checked without requiring a literal molecular duplicate of a cell.

1. Transition preservation
--------------------------
Let X be a biological MODEL's states, including its specified environment,
not every state of a real cell. Let Y be software states. Let phi map X to Y,
and let alpha map the permitted model interventions to software actions.
For a deterministic transition F_a and a translated transition G_alpha(a),
exact correspondence on domain D requires:

    phi(F_a(x)) = G_alpha(a)(phi(x))     for every (x,a) in D,

and preservation of whether the transition is enabled. A transition blocked
by a missing substrate cannot become enabled solely because a record naming
the substrate exists. If phi and alpha are bijections with corresponding
inverse relations, this is a state/action isomorphism on that domain. If phi
merges states, it is an abstraction; exactness is a separate question.

For stochastic dynamics the corresponding requirement compares the
pushforward of the full next-state probability distribution. Matching allowed
transitions alone does not determine timing, reaction rates, dwell times,
stationary weights, or responses outside the model's intervention domain.

2. An elementary necessary-and-sufficient condition
---------------------------------------------------
Fix an action and a target observable h(F_a(x)). A deterministic predictor
g(phi(x),a) that is correct for every x exists if and only if the target is
constant on every fiber of phi (states sharing the same representation).

Necessity: if phi(x1)=phi(x2), a function g receives identical arguments, so
it returns the same answer. If the two true targets differ, one answer must
be wrong. Sufficiency: when all targets on each fiber agree, define g on
that fiber to be that common target. Enabledness can be included in the
target to cover blocked actions.

For a finite uniformly weighted table, the minimum possible classification
errors from a fixed representation equal the sum over its fibers of
    fiber_size - largest_target_class_size.
This is a bound for that table and weighting, not a natural frequency claim.

Implication for this audit: a representation that ignores current substrate
identity, quantity, compartment, or activity can merge states with different
legal transitions. No amount of label substitution or downstream hashing
can repair that missing distinction. The distinction may remain recoverable
in the archived source; the claim concerns information used by the executing
path, not irreversible loss throughout the whole project.

3. Composition and environment
------------------------------
Two individually correct translations can compose incorrectly if they give a
shared resource two independent copies, consume a reusable catalyst, omit a
transport precondition, or silently change the shared environment. A coupled
model must preserve shared identities and specify its boundary exchanges.
The correspondence test therefore applies to composed transitions and traces,
not just isolated reaction records.

4. Multiple meanings of closure
-------------------------------
Full-state closure is F_trace(x)=x. Projected closure is
    pi(F_trace(x)) = pi(x)
for a declared observable pi. Projected closure can be scientifically useful
while the full system consumes substrates and releases products. Neither
definition establishes a privileged ladder length without a separate
derivation or a discriminating comparison over lengths and hypotheses.

Here closure means exact return after a finite trace. The supplied TLD
Lexicon I-XIV defines ladder closure differently: convergence to a stable
terminal under frozen rules, with an explicit error objective and eligibility
constraints. The reaction example does not silently redefine that endpoint
and is not a fresh TLD closure run. Likewise winner_N is a harmonic-mode
label, T_e is onset depth, and S_e is persistence depth; these are distinct.

5. Equal-information control
----------------------------
An independently implemented generic solver supplied the same state,
transition rules, and information should agree with an exactly translated
biological model. Such agreement supports translation fidelity. A benefit
from biology-derived structure would instead need to appear in model
construction, sample efficiency, action selection, transfer, robustness, or
predictive accuracy against a declared alternative that does not already
encode the same constraints. Neutral labels neither establish nor refute
that stronger benefit.

6. Scope of today's work
-----------------------
The reaction examples are finite inventory/state contracts grounded in
curated Reactome events. They do not estimate cell kinetics, prove a complete
cell/software isomorphism, establish software repair gain, or validate the
physical claims or preferred ladder length of TORUS/TLD. Mathematical
validity within a model and experimental validity of that model remain
separate evidence obligations.

Prior formal work (these concepts are not claimed as new theorems)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Rubenstein et al. (2017), Causal Consistency of Structural Equation Models.
https://proceedings.mlr.press/r15/rubenstein-17a.html
Geiger et al. (2025), Causal Abstraction: A Theoretical Foundation for
Mechanistic Interpretability. https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v26/23-0058.html
Haydarlou et al. (2016), BioASF: a framework for automatically generating
executable pathway models specified in BioPAX. DOI 10.1093/bioinformatics/btw250.

These sources motivate formal correspondence and executable translation.
The short fiber argument and the proposed tests above are stated explicitly
so their limited conclusions can be checked without an appeal to analogy.
